The Roadmap to 10 Million Lives by 2035

Every maneuverstarts with a compelling vision. For Nouricare Uganda ltd., that vision was simple yet radical: to eliminate a preventable crisis caused by malnutrition and become Africa’s leading partner in nutritional excellence. Our journey purposes to respond to a profound need, charting an ambitious course for regional impact.

An origin in urgent need

Our roots are grounded in the grim reality that malnutrition is systematically robbing our communities of their potential. Observing severe impact on vulnerable groups, especially the crisis of low birth weight among infants, we recognized the urgent need for an affordable nutritional product that offered superior quality, customization, and absolute efficacy. As a result, Nouricare reloaded, a scientifically formulated "Quadro-formula" derived from highquality local ingredients was created. Not as a generic supplement, but as a targeted nutritional solution rich in protein, omega-3 fatty acids, and essential vitamins, tailored to support life's most ritical stages. Our commitment to integrity and trust was embedded from day one: by producing every blend freshly made to order, we guarantee potency and build an unshakeable bond with the families that depend on us.

The future map

Our journey is guided by our core values; nutritional excellence, integrity and trust, vitality and impact, and commitment to sustainability. Our immediate focus is to consolidate leadership in Uganda and start on regional expansion that is;

• Establish Nouricare products as the leading prescribed nutritional supplements in Uganda, driven by partnerships with hospitals, health food stores, and pharmacies, we look to command a greater portion of the market share.

• Achieve a 99% customer satisfaction rate on freshness and potency by optimizing our unique made-on-demand production model.

• Finally, successfully launch distribution into two neighboring East African countries, proving our model's effectiveness across new markets.

By 2035, Nouricare will be synonymous with life-changing impact across East Africa. We want to be the most trusted and sustainably packaged nutritional partner across the region, committed to driving a measurable reduction in malnutrition and ensuring optimal health and vitality for over 10 million vulnerable individuals, including babies, children, breastfeeding mothers, and the elderly. We will continuously invest in Research & Development (R&D) to enhance our product lineage, maintain pioneering stance on sustainability and keep our promise of zero non-compostable waste through innovative packaging. Our journey is more than just business; it is about rewriting the health narrative for millions of people. Join us as a customer or partner, and help us build a nutritionally empowered future.

The Problem: Alarming global reality demands

There is a silent crisis of maternal and child malnutrition which is not just a regional issue but a global emergency threatening the future of over 19.8 million newborns worldwide annually. That’s one in every seven babies born globally with Low Birth Weight (LBW). In Uganda, this crisis is even more pronounced. A staggering 25.5% of newborns suffer LBW that is, one in four babies starts life facing an immediate, systemic disadvantage. This isn't just a number, it is a direct result of inadequate maternal nutrition, a failure that carries catastrophic, lifelong consequences. You must choose Nouricare today to protect the next generation.

What Happens If We Don't Act

If maternal malnutrition and the resulting LBW crisis are not addressed right now, the consequences are irreversible and devastating, affecting not only families but the entire nation: LBW infants are 20 to 40 times more likely to die within their first month of life than normal-weight babies i.e. Increased mortality. Whereas survivors may face an increased risk of permanent developmental and cognitive delays presenting as lower IQ, poor fetal nutrition "programs" the body for illness risking adult-onset chronic conditions like diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and hypertension. Stunted growth and impaired cognitive function lower educational attainment and reduced adult productivity, costing the country billions in lost human capital.

The Solution: Why Choose Nouricare?

  • Scientifically formulated with essential proteins, minerals, and Omega-3s.
  • Freshly made-to-order for maximum potency and absorption.
  • Customized variants for babies, mothers, elderly, and vulnerable groups.
  • Guaranteed nutrient accuracy through our made-on-demand process.
  • 100% plastic-free, compostable packaging for a healthier planet.
