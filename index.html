Who We Are

Welcome to Nouricare Uganda

Nouricare Uganda Ltd was established in 2025 as “Your Nutritional Partner.” We are committed to improving the health and nutrition of our communities through high-quality, scientifically formulated porridge products. Our focus is to provide fresh, ready-made nutritional blends that cater to different care needs - from breastfeeding mothers and children to the elderly and individuals with malnutrition.

We produce 100% plastic-free, home-compostable, and freshly made products that retain high potency and absorption for optimal nourishment. At Nouricare, we combine nature and science to deliver nutrition that truly makes a difference.

Our Vision & Mission

Empowering every generation with science-backed, naturally sourced nutrition for a healthier Africa.

Our Vision

To be the leading nutritional partner in Africa, ensuring optimal health and vitality across every stage of life - from infancy to the elderly - through superior, tailored, and naturally sourced nutritional products.

By 2035, Nouricare Uganda Ltd aims to be the most trusted and sustainably packaged nutritional brand in East Africa, positively impacting over 10 million vulnerable individuals.

Our Mission

  • Customizing Nutrition: Developing scientifically formulated blends like the Quadro-formula to meet diverse dietary needs.
  • Ensuring Efficacy & Quality: Producing high-potency products made fresh using Legume, Omega 3, Cereal, and Lactose.
  • Committing to Sustainability: Packaging all products in 100% plastic-free, home-compostable materials.
